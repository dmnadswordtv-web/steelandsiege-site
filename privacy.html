Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

The Steel & Siege uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Steel & Siege YouTube channel to upload, schedule and update that channel's own videos.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's channel, set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, visibility and publish times, and read back the status of those videos.

What the app stores

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. The app does not collect, sell or share personal data, and it does not access any other user's data.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service; see the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Contact the owner through the Steel & Siege YouTube channel.
